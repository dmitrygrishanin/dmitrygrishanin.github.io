Support for Astra: Natal Chart

Welcome to the official support page for Astra, a personal astrology app for natal charts, current transits, and daily reflective insights.

Frequently Asked Questions

I cannot sign in with Apple or Google.

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Make sure you are using the latest version of Astra.
  • Close and reopen the app, then try again.
  • Confirm that the selected Apple or Google account is active.

My natal chart or daily forecast is not loading.

  • Check your connection and try again after a short pause.
  • Open your profile and verify your birth date, birth time, and birth place.
  • If your exact birth time is unknown, enable the “Birth time unknown” option.

How can I correct my birth information?

Open your profile in Astra and edit your birth date, birth time, or birth place. The app will recalculate the relevant chart data after the update.

How can I delete my account?

Open your profile in Astra and choose Delete Account. If you cannot access the app, contact support from the email address associated with your account.

When will I receive a response?

Support requests are usually answered within 1–2 business days, Monday through Friday, Central European Time.
