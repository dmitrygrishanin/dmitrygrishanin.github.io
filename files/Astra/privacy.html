Privacy Policy

Application: Astra: Natal Chart

This Privacy Policy explains how Astra: Natal Chart (“Astra”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), operated by independent developer Dmytro Hryshanin, collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Astra iOS application and related backend services.

Astra provides astrology-based content for entertainment, information, and personal reflection. Astrology is not a scientifically proven method of prediction.

1. Information We Collect

Account and authentication information

  • A technical account identifier assigned by Astra.
  • Your display name.
  • Your selected sign-in provider, such as Apple or Google.
  • The provider account identifier needed to recognize your account.
  • Your email address and verification status when supplied by the sign-in provider.
  • Session identifiers and session expiration information used to keep you signed in securely.

Birth profile information

  • Birth date.
  • Birth time, or an indication that the exact birth time is unknown.
  • Birth place name.
  • Latitude, longitude, time zone, and related time-zone offset data for the selected birth place.

Astrological and generated content

  • Calculated natal chart data, including planetary placements, houses, aspects, and related calculation results.
  • Daily transit calculations and personalized forecast data.
  • Generated natal chart interpretations and daily reflective text.
  • Generation status, timestamps, and technical error information.

Usage, diagnostics, and device information

  • App interaction events, such as onboarding completion, screen opens, authentication results, and feature usage.
  • App language and an internal Astra user identifier used for analytics.
  • Crash reports, diagnostics, app version, operating system version, device model, and similar technical information.
  • Network and server log information, which may include IP address, request timestamps, endpoint paths, and internal identifiers.

Support information

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, app version, iOS version, device model, and an internal Astra user identifier.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your Astra account.
  • To authenticate you through Apple or Google.
  • To calculate your natal chart and current planetary transits.
  • To generate and display personalized interpretations and daily forecasts.
  • To save your profile, chart, forecast history, and preferences.
  • To provide local reminder notifications that you configure on your device.
  • To diagnose failures, prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve performance.
  • To understand aggregate feature usage and improve the product.
  • To answer support requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. AI-Generated Content

Astra uses an artificial intelligence service to generate some natal chart interpretations and daily text. For this purpose, Astra may send derived astrological context—such as planetary placements, houses, aspects, transit information, language preference, and whether the birth time is unknown—to the AI provider.

Astra is designed not to send Apple or Google sign-in tokens, Astra session tokens, passwords, or payment information to the AI provider. AI-generated content may be stored in Astra so it can be displayed again without regenerating it.

4. Third-Party Services

Astra relies on service providers that process information only as needed to provide their services:

  • Apple Sign in with Apple — authentication. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In — authentication. Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics — usage analytics and crash diagnostics. Firebase Privacy and Security
  • OpenAI — generation of personalized astrology text. OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Heroku / Salesforce — backend hosting, application logs, and database infrastructure. Salesforce Privacy Statement

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own advertising or cross-app tracking purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

Account and astrology data are stored on backend infrastructure used by Astra. Data transmitted between the app and the backend is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, access controls, and authentication measures designed to protect information.

No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your Apple or Google account and your device.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account and profile information while your Astra account remains active and as needed to provide the service. Technical logs and diagnostics may be retained for a limited period for security, troubleshooting, and operational purposes.

You can delete your account directly in the app by opening your profile and selecting Delete Account. Account deletion removes the account and associated application data from the active Astra database. Limited residual information may remain temporarily in security logs, system caches, or backups where technically necessary or legally required.

If you cannot access the app, request deletion by emailing dmitrygrishanin@gmail.com.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or a copy of your personal information, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing.

To submit a privacy request, contact dmitrygrishanin@gmail.com. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account.

8. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable, Astra processes personal information on one or more of these bases:

  • performance of the service you request;
  • legitimate interests in operating, securing, diagnosing, and improving Astra;
  • your consent, where consent is required;
  • compliance with legal obligations.

9. International Data Processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Where required, we rely on appropriate contractual and legal safeguards for international data transfers.

10. Children’s Privacy

Astra is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so that we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Astra’s features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version will remain available on this page, and the effective date will be updated.
